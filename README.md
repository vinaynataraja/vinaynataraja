I am an **Engineering Manager and Data Architect** at British Telecom, driving strategic data initiatives across cloud and on-premise platforms.

My work focuses on designing and operating scalable **Data Lake, Lakehouse, and Data Mesh** architectures that enable analytics, data science, and AI at enterprise scale.

I am currently deepening my expertise in **AI Platform Architecture**, with a particular interest in building secure, governed, and production-ready AI systems on trusted data foundations.
